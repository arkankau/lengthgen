# Basin-Energy Extension: Multi-Perspective Research Synthesis

Method: `.claude/research.md` (5-perspective scan -> contradiction map -> synthesis -> self peer-review), grounded with live web search/fetch rather than run as pure prompting.

Scope question: is extending the null-attractor diagnostic with explicit basin-energy scoring (`E_b = -cos(h, c_b)`, `F = E - TS`, basin competition) a credible, publishable next step, and what does prior art say about how to do it right.

## Stage 1 — Five Perspectives

**Practitioner (someone building steering/detection systems hands-on)**
Two-sentence position: Refusal-direction steering is well-trodden and easy to implement, but practitioners report it's brittle in exactly the way this project already found — steering a "refusal direction" into generation degrades fluency or fails to redirect content, because the direction that separates classes post-hoc is not the same as a direction that causally controls generation when pushed. Strongest evidence: "Activation Steering Compromises LLM Safety" and "What Drives Representation Steering?" (arXiv:2604.08524) both document steering-induced incoherence and interrogate *why* push-in-direction fails even when the direction linearly separates train data.
Unique contribution: the failure mode this project already hit (`gpt2_family_behavior_failure_note.md`) is a known, named phenomenon in the steering literature, not a one-off implementation bug — which is good news for the paper's honesty framing.

**Researcher/academic**
Two-sentence position: The energy/Hopfield reading of attention is mainstream and load-bearing (Ramsauer et al.'s modern Hopfield networks -> Energy Transformer -> 2026 "Transformers as Intrinsic Optimizers" reframing the whole forward pass as energy minimization), so grounding null-attractor logits in `E = -q.k/sqrt(d) - b/beta`, `p ~ exp(-beta E)` is defensible, reviewer-legible math, not a metaphor stretch. Strongest evidence: arXiv:2411.08590 (Hopfield-Fenchel-Young Networks) shows softmax/sparsemax/entmax attention variants are *all* special cases of energy-based retrieval — this is exactly the theoretical anchor `thermodynamic_attractor_derivations.md` already cites.
Unique contribution: cosine-similarity-as-energy against class anchors is not novel in isolation — it is essentially the "Class Typical Matching" OOD-detection method (OpenReview QuXApqb8Yl / arXiv:2306.14920) — so the paper should cite it as precedent for "cosine-to-anchor as a rigorous energy proxy" rather than presenting the formula as original.

**Skeptic (strongest good-faith case against the plan)**
Two-sentence position: The single biggest threat to `E_safe = -cos(h, a_safe)` is that refusal is now empirically known to be **not one-dimensional** — Wollschläger et al. (ICML 2025, "The Geometry of Refusal in LLMs: Concept Cones and Representational Independence") show refusal is mediated by a multi-dimensional polyhedral cone of directions, and that orthogonality does not imply independence under intervention. A single-anchor cosine energy will therefore be a lossy, possibly misleading projection of a genuinely multi-dimensional basin, and a reviewer who knows this literature will ask why a scalar cosine score is the right observable at all.
Evidence glossed over by the practitioner/researcher framing: "energy" computed against one centroid can look clean (a smooth basin-competition curve) purely because a single direction captures *some* variance, while still misrepresenting the geometry — this is a real risk of a false sense of rigor, not just a cosmetic concern.

**Incentives analyst**
Two-sentence position: Interpretability-flavored jailbreak-detection papers are currently rewarded (workshop tracks, safety-adjacent venues, red-teaming vendor interest) for *novel-sounding mechanistic framing* more than for incremental probing-classifier AUROC numbers, which creates pressure to over-claim the "thermodynamic" angle as more than a re-derivation of existing probing/OOD techniques in physics language. The safest posture, and the one that protects credibility, is exactly what `key_paper_move.md` already does — explicitly cite the closest overlapping methods (probing classifiers get 80%+ accuracy per arXiv:2510.06594) and position the contribution as the *diagnostic observable set* (entropy, spectral gap, susceptibility, basin competition) rather than as beating those baselines on raw detection accuracy.
Unique contribution: the paper's existing "avoid" list (do not claim defense, do not claim null mass = safety) is unusually good hygiene relative to the field's typical incentive gradient — worth explicitly noting in the related-work section as a deliberate scope decision, since it's not the norm.

**Historian (analogous past situations)**
Two-sentence position: This maps closely onto Boltzmann-machine/Hopfield-network history — the field spent the 1980s-90s learning that a network's energy landscape is a genuinely useful diagnostic lens (spin-glass phase transitions, storage capacity limits) long before anyone could reliably *sculpt* that landscape to do new work on demand; detection preceded control by years, and control required qualitatively new machinery (learned energy functions, not hand-picked centroids). Historical parallel: attention-sink research went through the same two-stage arc very recently — sinks were characterized as a diagnostic curiosity (2023-2024) before anyone showed how to suppress or exploit them for hallucination detection with a validated method (arXiv:2604.10697, 2026).
Lesson for this project: don't rush the "control" half. The historical pattern rewards papers that nail the diagnostic-observable stage cleanly and explicitly flag control as future work — exactly the posture `key_paper_move.md` already takes, and exactly why basin energy should ship as a **diagnostic figure**, not a defense claim, in the next iteration.

## Stage 2 — Contradiction Map

- **Direct conflict:** Researcher perspective says single-anchor cosine energy is legitimate and precedented (CTM/OOD literature); Skeptic says a single anchor is provably insufficient given refusal-cone evidence. **Resolution:** both are right at different scopes — a single-anchor cosine energy is a valid, precedented *diagnostic scalar*, but it should be explicitly labeled as a first-order projection of a higher-dimensional basin, with a stated limitation, not sold as "the" refusal energy. This is a labeling/claims-discipline issue, not a reason to abandon the metric.
- **Which side has stronger evidence:** Skeptic's citation (ICML 2025, peer-reviewed, direct refutation of the single-direction assumption) is stronger than the researcher's precedent (which validates the *method*, not the *sufficiency* of one direction). The resolving question: does adding a second/third anchor axis (e.g., top-k PCA directions of the refusal-minus-unsafe difference across many prompt pairs, not just one pair) change basin separation meaningfully? That single ablation would settle the disagreement empirically.
- **Universal agreement:** All five perspectives agree that entropy/spectral-gap/basin-competition *observables* beyond raw classification accuracy are the actual novel contribution, and that this project's negative-control framing (null mass != safety) is unusually disciplined and worth keeping.
- **Gap none of the five addressed:** None of the perspectives found prior art that reports a basin-competition table (`E_safe` vs `E_unsafe` vs `E_benign` jointly, with entropy over basin occupancy) as a *paper figure* the way `thermodynamic_attractor_derivations.md` §7 proposes — the closest analogues (OOD "energy barrier", refusal cones, latent trajectory papers) each report one or two of these pieces but not the joint basin-competition table. That gap is this project's actual opening, not the cosine-energy formula itself.

## Stage 3 — Synthesis Briefing

**Executive summary:** The planned basin-energy extension is well-grounded — attention-as-Hopfield-energy is mainstream theory, and cosine-to-anchor scoring has direct OOD-detection precedent — but the credible version of it must (a) explicitly cite and differentiate from the closest 2025-2026 papers (refusal cones, latent refusal trajectories, attention-head specialization, representation-steering failure analyses) rather than presenting the energy formulation as novel in isolation, and (b) report basin geometry with an explicit acknowledgment that refusal is multidimensional, ideally validated with a quick multi-anchor ablation. The actual novel contribution to claim is the **joint basin-competition table + entropy/spectral-gap coupling**, not the existence of a cosine-energy score by itself.

**Five key findings, ranked by reliability:**
1. *(High — peer-reviewed, directly on point)* Refusal is mediated by a multi-dimensional cone, not a single direction (Wollschläger et al., ICML 2025). Supported by skeptic, contested by nobody. **Action: any single-anchor `a_safe` must be labeled a lossy projection; ideally validate against a 2nd/3rd basis direction.**
2. *(High — multiple converging sources)* Attention-as-energy/Hopfield-retrieval is an actively developing, legitimate theoretical frame through 2026 (Energy Transformer, Hopfield-Fenchel-Young Networks, "Transformers as Intrinsic Optimizers"). Supported by researcher and historian. **Action: cite this lineage explicitly in Background, not just the original Hopfield/attention-sink knowledge file.**
3. *(Medium-high)* Steering along a refusal-like direction reliably risking incoherent/degenerate output is a documented, named phenomenon, not unique to this project's implementation (arXiv OpenReview 987905e2..., arXiv:2604.08524). Supported by practitioner. **Action: reframe the GPT-2 generation-intervention failure note to cite this literature — it converts "our intervention failed" into "our result replicates a known failure mode," which is a stronger paper claim.**
4. *(Medium)* Head-local / component-local safety signals surviving jailbreak attacks, while global behavior collapses, is now independently replicated (arXiv:2606.28153, Adversarially-Compromised-Heads vs Safety-Aligned-Heads). Supported by researcher, consistent with this project's own head-selection results (`gpt2_medium_head_selection_note.md`). **Action: cite as convergent evidence for Claim 6 in `claims_and_evidence.md`; consider adopting their Overlap-Coefficient (OVL) metric as a more standard alternative/complement to the current simple mean-difference `sep`.**
5. *(Medium — one very close paper, use carefully)* "Tracing the Dynamics of Refusal" (arXiv:2605.02958) already combines entropy + energy-like quantities + trajectory geometry for jailbreak detection with reported AUROC ~0.85-0.95, FRR <5%, on Llama/Mistral/Qwen/DeepSeek. Supported by researcher; flagged by incentives analyst as the paper most likely to make this project's contribution look incremental if not explicitly differentiated. **Action: this needs a direct paragraph in related work — this project's distinguishing angle is attention-internal (m_null, spectral gap, susceptibility on GPT-2-family) versus their hidden-state-trajectory framing on larger instruction-tuned models; say so explicitly, do not let a reviewer draw the comparison first.**

**Non-obvious connection visible only across all five perspectives:** The historian's "detection precedes control by years" pattern plus the skeptic's "refusal is multidimensional" finding together suggest the *right-sized* next experiment is not "make the semantic attractor work" (control) but "measure whether basin energy against multiple candidate anchors is even coherent" (diagnostic) — i.e., test whether `E_safe`/`E_unsafe`/`E_benign` computed from a single mean-difference anchor agrees with a version computed from, say, the top-2 PCA directions of several refusal/unsafe prompt pairs. If they diverge, that itself is a publishable diagnostic finding ("basin energy is anchor-fragile"), consistent with the paper's honest-negative-result posture.

**Concrete action recommendation:** Implement the basin-energy diagnostic (as scoped in the prior turn: `thermosafety/basin_energy.py` + `scripts/evaluate_basin_energy.py`) with two additions motivated by this research: (1) compute `E_safe`/`E_unsafe`/`E_benign` from *both* a single mean-difference anchor and a multi-pair PCA-direction anchor, report both, and (2) add a short related-work subsection explicitly citing and differentiating from arXiv:2605.02958, arXiv:2502.17420 (refusal cones), and arXiv:2606.28153 (head specialization) before running further generation-intervention experiments.

**Open question that would most change the picture:** Does the single-anchor and multi-anchor basin energy actually diverge on this project's own GPT-2-family data? If they agree closely, the simpler single-anchor formulation is vindicated for this model scale and the paper can proceed with the original plan. If they diverge, the paper needs a section on anchor sensitivity as a limitation/finding.
